My top 5 restaurants

📍 Roppongi

Kinka Sushi Bar Izakaya

My absolute favorite sushi chain, which comes originally from Canada. Kinka always had a cozy atmosphere and fantastic food. I highly recommend the omakase with flamed fish.

📍 Shibuya

Ichiran Ramen

The best ramen chain you can find in Japan. They only serve one dish but it is amazing and just a classic: Tonkotsu Ramen.

📍 Shibuya

Shabu-yo

Shabu-yo is an all-you-can-eat hot pot restaurant. You can go to a buffet and get veggies, noodles, rice, desserts and more.
